I build the test systems that bring LiDAR sensors from prototype to production at Waymo. I design automated test equipment, set up final assembly and calibration lines with manufacturers in the US and overseas, and track failures back to their root cause, whether that's hardware design or software.

Before Waymo, I studied robotics at Johns Hopkins. While there I worked on a robotic grasping dataset, a computer-vision system that found free library seats, and a UR5 arm that plays Jenga. I grew up in Taiwan and studied EECS at National Tsing Hua University.
